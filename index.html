About

Founder's Office · PM & Tech @ Humann · Hyderabad

I'm a CSE graduate who knew pretty early on that I didn't just want to build products. I wanted to help shape them.

That's exactly why I took a Founder's Office role at an early-stage startup. I saw it as the fastest way to learn how products are actually built, decisions are made, and businesses grow. What I didn't realize was that "Founder's Office" is startup code for "congratulations, you're now responsible for whatever needs to get done."

fun fact ✦

Gen Z rep of the office. Still waiting for the official job description.

hats worn 🎩

PM · Designer · Analyst · Leader · Salesperson · Recruiter · Operator · Support · Problem-solver

Being in a Founder's Office is basically startup speed-running, you don't pick a lane, you learn the whole map. No cape, no superpowers, just an ever-expanding job description and the ability to switch between ten tabs, five priorities, and three roles before lunch.

Working in a mental health startup has shaped the way I think about products. Users aren't just metrics on a dashboard, they're real people navigating real challenges. It's made me more intentional about empathy, accessibility, and designing with the human behind the screen in mind.

What excites me most about product is that it's where technology, business, and human behavior collide. I love connecting dots, questioning assumptions, and turning messy ideas into products that genuinely solve problems.

Outside of work: devoted F1 fan, food lover cursed with a disappointingly small appetite, sketch-and-paint person. Also: badminton, tennis, basketball, Modern Family on loop, BTS, and reality TV I'm absolutely watching ironically. For now, happily building products and growing into the kind of PM who never loses sight of the humans behind the metrics.
