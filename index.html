About
Me
hi, i'm
Sufia.
currently
Tech & Product Manager @ Humann · Hyderabad

I've always been the kind of person who wants to know how things work, why people do what they do, and whether there's a better way to do something.

I studied Computer Science Engineering, but somewhere along the way, I realised I was just as interested in the people, decisions and little details behind the technology as I was in the technology itself.

I like starting with a question and following it somewhere unexpected. Sometimes that turns into a product idea. Sometimes a new project. Sometimes a rabbit hole I absolutely did not need to go down at 2 a.m.

I'm a little analytical, a little creative, and probably more curious than is practical. I enjoy making things, challenging assumptions, connecting seemingly unrelated dots and learning by doing. I don't particularly enjoy staying in one box.

birthday girl, frosting and all
fun fact ✦

My brain has approximately 27 tabs open. Product ideas, F1 strategy, random questions and a song on repeat. All equally important.

A few things that make me, me: An F1 race weekend is a perfectly valid personality trait. I sketch and paint when I can, have a soft spot for good design, and believe biryani deserves its own food group. Modern Family is always playing somewhere in the background. BTS, badminton, tennis and reality TV (strictly for research, obviously) also make the list.

I'm figuring things out as I go, building along the way, and trying to stay interested in the world around me. That's probably the most consistent thing about me.
